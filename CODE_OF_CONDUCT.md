# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We want the Watchman community to be welcoming, respectful, and useful for everyone.

People should be able to report issues, ask questions, suggest ideas, and contribute without being treated poorly.

## Expected Behavior

Please:

- Be respectful and patient
- Give helpful feedback
- Assume good intent
- Stay focused on the issue or contribution
- Be kind to beginners

## Unacceptable Behavior

Please do not:

- Harass, insult, or threaten others
- Use hateful or discriminatory language
- Share private information without permission
- Spam issues, pull requests, or discussions
- Attack people instead of discussing the work

## Reporting

If you see behavior that does not fit this code of conduct, contact the project maintainers privately at [alnoman.dhoni@gmail.com](mailto:alnoman.dhoni@gmail.com) or [wrafiz.k@gmail.com](mailto:wrafiz.k@gmail.com), or reach out to us on our [Discord server](https://discord.gg/RSrx4zs2Hh).

Reports will be reviewed and handled as fairly as possible.

## Scope

This code of conduct applies to Watchman project spaces, including GitHub issues, pull requests, discussions, and other project-related communication.
